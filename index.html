What Tender & Licence Actually Does

We're a licensing and compliance consultancy based in Lucknow, with a second office in Gurugram. The work is straightforward: businesses come to us when they need a government licence, a registration, a certification, or help filing a tender - and we handle the paperwork, the submission, the follow-up, and the back-and-forth with the department until the job is done.

Most of our clients are contractors, small business owners, and firms that know what they want to do but don't have the time or patience to navigate government processes themselves. We've built our practice around the filings we do most often - PSARA, electrical contractor licences, FSSAI, GeM registration, railway and state tenders - because doing the same types of applications regularly means we know exactly what each department expects and where files typically get stuck.

We don't guarantee approvals (no honest consultant can), and we don't claim to have special access. What we do is make sure your file is complete, correctly structured, and submitted properly the first time, which is genuinely the biggest factor in how fast things move.
